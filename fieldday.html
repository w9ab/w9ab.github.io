What is Field Day?

ARRL Field Day is an annual amateur radio event that brings radio operators together throughout the United States and Canada.

During Field Day, amateur radio clubs and individual operators set up stations and make contacts with other Field Day stations. The event combines on-the-air operating with emergency preparedness, technical skills, experimentation, community outreach and the social side of amateur radio.

For MARC, Field Day is also an opportunity for club members and visitors to spend time together, operate radio and share the amateur radio hobby with the Michiana community.

For complete rules and official Field Day information, visit the ARRL Field Day website.
