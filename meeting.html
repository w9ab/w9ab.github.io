The October exercise

From a communications plan
to a ready-to-use radio.

  1. Read the ICS-205.Identify channel names, receive and transmit frequencies, tones, and channel assignments.
  2. Hand-program your HT.Enter the repeater and simplex channels using your radio’s front-panel controls—without a computer, programming cable, CHIRP, or codeplug software.
  3. Demonstrate your settings.Recall the channels and verify the frequency, offset, and tone information against the plan with the workshop evaluator.

What to bring

  • Your handheld radio and antenna
  • A fully charged battery
  • The operating manual or a saved digital copy
  • A phone and headphones if you’d like to follow a video

Watch your radio’s programming reference before the meeting, then bring it along and practice with us.

Document your training

ARES task-book skills

Participants who successfully complete the exercise will receive a training record documenting the skills demonstrated:

  • Program a tone into an HT.
  • Program frequency and offset into a radio.

Keep the record with your ARES training documentation. Task-book sign-off follows your local ARES evaluator’s requirements.
