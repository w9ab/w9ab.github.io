This month's topic

What belongs in a radio Go-Bag?

Whether you're supporting a marathon, a bike ride, or another community event, a well-prepared kit helps you communicate reliably and stay comfortable throughout your assignment.

We'll draw on lessons from the Chicago Marathon, Marine Mud Run, Apple Cider Century, and North Liberty Lights of Liberty.

Topics we'll cover

  • Radios, spare batteries, antennas, and headsets
  • Chargers, power sources, and weather protection
  • Identification, communications plans, and paperwork
  • Food, water, clothing, and personal supplies
  • Keeping your equipment organized and ready to deploy
Come prepared

Bring your own ideas.

If you already have a Go-Bag or portable station kit, consider bringing it to share your setup and the lessons you've learned in the field.

Lights of Liberty begins November 20, the day after our meeting—an excellent reminder to have your radio programmed and equipment ready.
